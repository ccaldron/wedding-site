Most flights · PHX

Phoenix Sky Harbor

Prescott is almost exactly two hours from Phoenix Sky Harbor without traffic. Plan to rent a car or join a carpool.

Closest airport · PRC

Prescott Regional Airport

Small regional airport in Prescott with United Express service from Denver and Los Angeles. confirm 2027 schedule before publishing

  1. Leave Sky Harbor and head north on I-17 toward Flagstaff.
  2. Take exit 262 at Cordes Junction onto AZ-69, and follow it about 34 miles into Prescott.
  3. In Prescott, make your way to Senator Highway heading south. Your GPS will route you through town.
  4. Turn onto Friendly Pines Road from Senator Highway (not Marapai) and follow it to camp at 933 E Friendly Pines Rd.

Frequently asked questions

What is the dress code?

We want everyone to feel comfortable and warm. Friday’s ceremony and the party afterward are semi-formal. Saturday is comfortable active wear. The grounds are uneven and hilly in areas, so we don’t recommend heels.

Do I have to stay on site, or can I get a hotel or come for the day?

There will be enough cabins for all guests to stay on site, and we request that everyone attending stays on site.

What are the cabins like?

Cabins fit about six people in bunk-bed style accommodations. They’re rustic and basic, but all have an indoor bathroom with a shower and sink, plus a heater and a fan. Pack bedding (sleeping bags recommended) and toiletries.

Out-of-town travelers can reach out to contact name for help with bedding. If any friends or family can bring extra sleeping bags to share, we’d greatly appreciate it.

Answer needed

How much will this cost, and what does it include?

We’re hoping everyone attending can chip in $175 toward accommodations and food for the weekend. It’s meant to cover everything except bedding and transportation.

We chose a summer camp style wedding so we could maximize time with all of you without breaking the bank, and we’re subsidizing as much of the weekend as we can. We don’t want cost to be the reason you can’t attend, so please talk with us if price is a stressor.

What will the food be like?

Camp-style meals are provided from Friday dinner through Sunday breakfast. Snacks, coffee and drinks will be available to grab anytime.

Will there be cell service and Wi-Fi?

The camp has public Wi-Fi, and you should have reliable cell service during your stay.

What will the weather be like?

March forecasts suggest the 50s–60s during the day and the 30s–40s at night. Bring warm layers.

Do I have to be there at a certain time on Friday?

Our draft schedule has guests arriving around 1:00 PM. confirm latest arrival time

Do I have to do ALL the activities?

Gosh, no! We want this to be a fun and relaxing weekend. Sign up for what sounds fun, and know you have a guilt-free pass to skip anything that doesn’t.

Is this a kid-friendly event?

100%! There will be tons of activities for kids. We’re also hoping to set up a Kids Corner (think cruise-ship daycare) on Saturday and Sunday, and we’re looking for volunteers or paid babysitters to give moms and dads a break. Interested in helping? Reach out to contact name.

Can I bring an extra +1 or someone to help with childcare?

Absolutely! We want this to be a fun family trip. If you’d like a family member or childcare helper to stay with you on site, we’d be happy to have them. They’ll be charged as a camp attendee and are welcome at all events, but won’t be assigned a team unless you’d like them to be.

Sunday is Easter. Will there be an egg hunt or a church service?

We’ll have an Easter egg hunt! There won’t be a church service.
